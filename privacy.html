Cleaft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Cleaft is a free iPhone app. It does not require an account and does not include ads, analytics, or a developer-operated backend. Cleaft does not send your photos, videos, files, scan results, or choices to its developer.

Access you control

Cleaft asks for Photos permission only when you start a photo scan. If you grant limited access, it analyzes only the photos you selected. For documents, you choose specific folders through Apple's Files picker. Cleaft stores security-scoped folder bookmarks and your “keep” choices locally on your device so you can continue using the app. You can remove a selected folder in the app, or revoke Photos access in iPhone Settings.

On-device analysis and iCloud

Similarity analysis and content hashing run on your device. An optional deep scan may request originals from Apple's iCloud services after you choose it. Cleaft does not operate those services. Deleting iCloud-backed content may also remove it from your other devices through Apple's synchronization.

Deletion

Nothing is deleted by scanning or swiping. Deletion requires a final review and confirmation. Photos deletions use Apple's Photos framework, and file deletions use the selected file provider. Cleaft reports failures and does not count failed items as deleted.
