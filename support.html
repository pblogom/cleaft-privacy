Cleaft Support

Cleaft reviews your Photos library and only the Files folders you select. A scan or swipe never deletes anything; deletion requires final review and confirmation.

If a selected folder is unavailable, remove it and choose it again from Files. If Photos access is limited, use “Choose more photos” in Cleaft or adjust access in iPhone Settings. Deep scan may download originals from iCloud.

Getting things back

Photos and videos you delete with Cleaft go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. Files you delete are removed from their folder right away and cannot be recovered in Cleaft; for iCloud Drive, check Recently Deleted in the Files app or on iCloud.com. Back up anything you can't replace before deleting.
